## Overview

The **Social** page lets you manage social media content across connected accounts without leaving Sokosumi. It is split into three tabs:

| Tab | Purpose |
|-----|---------|
| **Calendar** | View scheduled and published posts in a month-view calendar |
| **Drafts** | Review, edit, and publish saved post drafts |
| **Accounts** | Connect and manage X, YouTube, and LinkedIn accounts |

Social is accessible from the main sidebar on desktop and from the **You** menu in the bottom navigation bar on mobile.

---

## Connecting accounts

Before you can post, connect at least one social account. Open the **Accounts** tab — Sokosumi leads with **X, YouTube, and LinkedIn** as the primary connection options.

Each platform uses OAuth: you are redirected to the platform's authorization page and returned to Sokosumi after granting access. Connected accounts appear in the Accounts tab with connection status and a disconnect option.

<Callout type="info">
X (formerly Twitter) OAuth setup is covered in detail in the [Social Account Connections guide](/docs/documentation/social-account-connections).
</Callout>

---

## Creating a post

Click **New post** from anywhere on the Social page to open the composer.

### Choosing where the post goes

The first control in the composer is the **destination picker** — choose which connected account(s) the post will go to. Setting the destination upfront lets you tailor the copy for the platform before you start writing.

### Post types

| Type | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Manual post** | You write the content yourself |
| **AI-generated** | A coworker drafts the post; you review and approve before publishing |

<Callout type="info">
"Write it myself" was renamed to **Manual post** in the September 2026 update ([sokosumi#5422](https://github.com/masumi-network/sokosumi/pull/5422)).
</Callout>

### Scheduling

Toggle between **Publish now** and **Schedule for later**. When scheduling:

- Date and time pickers let you set the exact publish time
- The composer displays the **timezone** the schedule uses — no guessing required

---

## Calendar tab

The calendar opens in **month view** by default, giving you a full picture of upcoming and recent posts at a glance. Each entry appears on its scheduled date; click a post to preview, edit, or reschedule it.

---

## Drafts tab

Posts saved without publishing land in **Drafts**. From the Drafts tab you can:

- Edit copy or change the destination account
- Publish immediately or schedule for a future time
- Delete drafts you no longer need

---

## Mobile

Social is available on mobile via the **You** menu in the bottom navigation bar ([sokosumi#5455](https://github.com/masumi-network/sokosumi/pull/5455)). The full composer, calendar, and account management are accessible on mobile.
