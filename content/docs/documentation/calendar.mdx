## Overview

The **Workspace Calendar** (`/calendar`) surfaces all scheduled work in your workspace: task schedules, project milestones, and ledger-backed events pulled from Core. It supports both v1 and v2 schedule formats, and annotates each event with a source and time accuracy indicator.

As of 2026-09-13, the calendar supports **interactive occurrence management** — you can drag, reschedule, and edit individual occurrences or entire series without affecting unrelated events.

---

## Views

| View | Description |
|---|---|
| **Month** | High-level overview of all scheduled events for the month |
| **Week** | Day-by-day breakdown of the current week |
| **Agenda** | Flat chronological list of upcoming scheduled items |

Switch between views using the controls at the top of the page.

---

## Filters

Filters are backed by URL parameters — a filtered view can be bookmarked or shared directly.

| Filter | Description |
|---|---|
| **Project** | Show schedules from a specific project only |
| **Status** | Filter events by task status |
| **Assignee** | Show only tasks assigned to a specific Coworker |

## Occurrence Management

Calendar events that belong to a recurring series support three editing operations. These let you adjust individual occurrences without touching the rest of the series.

### Drag and Move an Occurrence

Drag any occurrence to a new time slot on the calendar. Only that occurrence moves — the rest of the series stays on its original schedule.

<Callout type="info">
Dragging preserves the occurrence's duration. To change the duration, use the reschedule dialog instead.
</Callout>

### Reschedule an Occurrence

Click an occurrence and choose **Reschedule this event** to open the reschedule dialog. You can change the start time, end time, and date for that occurrence only.

The rescheduled occurrence is stored as an exception to the series rule. Other occurrences are unaffected.

### Manage a Series

Click an occurrence and choose **Edit series** to open the series management dialog. From here you can:

- Change the recurrence pattern (frequency, interval, end date)
- Update the title, assignee, or status for the whole series

**Revision safety:** When you save a series change, occurrences that have already been individually rescheduled or completed are preserved as-is. Only future, unmodified occurrences are updated to the new series rule. This prevents past exceptions from being silently overwritten.

<Callout type="warning">
Editing the series does not retroactively apply changes to past occurrences. If you need to correct a past occurrence, edit it individually.
</Callout>

## Schedule Accuracy

Each calendar event carries a **time accuracy** indicator derived from the ledger.

| Indicator | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `complete` | Schedule is fully ledger-backed — set explicitly and confirmed |
| `partial` | Schedule was reconciled from history and may not reflect the most recent change |

Accuracy indicators are especially relevant for tasks migrated from v1 schedule format, where some timing information may have been inferred rather than recorded.

## Known Limitations

- Dense recurring schedules may hit pagination bounds — in progress as a follow-up to SOK-881.
- Calendar day grouping is not yet timezone-consistent for all users.
- Dragging an occurrence across a month boundary in Month view is not yet supported — use the reschedule dialog instead.
